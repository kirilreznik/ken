# Details spot-check index

The stories and similar names for the name sheet were written in 38 batches (PRD name-detail §6.5, most given in 2024 first). Batches 1–20 cover the first 863 deck names (plus entries for names the Jewish-sector rule later left out of the deck); batches 21–38 cover the 1,074 names added to the deck on 2026-11-13 (CBS names also listed on Israeli name sites, and the formerly held names), again most given since 2000 first. Where a fact is uncertain the story says כנראה or אולי. Each batch has a spot-check file with 10 random entries, seeded by the batch number so the same 10 come up every time (`node scripts/names-content/spot-check-details.mjs <batch>`).

**Please read at least 3 of them.** A good spread: batch 1 (the most common names, seen most often), one middle batch with Arabic and Yiddish names (batch 11 or 19), and batch 20 (the rarest names, where a wrong fact is least likely to be noticed). For the names added on 2026-11-13, read batch 21 (the most common of them), one of 27–31 (many Yiddish, Russian and older Israeli names) and batch 38 (the rarest). Mark each row OK or write what to change; the overall picture (coverage, null stories, symmetry fills, drops) is in [DETAILS-REPORT.md](DETAILS-REPORT.md).
