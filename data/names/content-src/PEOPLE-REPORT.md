## Rules

- Candidate: a human (Q5) whose given name is this name, or whose Hebrew label starts with it, with a Hebrew Wikipedia article and a Hebrew description.
- Israelis first, then Hebrew Wikipedia page views (last 60 days), then the number of Wikipedia sitelinks.
- Left out automatically: politicians and anyone who held an office or has a party; military, intelligence, diplomats, activists; anyone convicted or described with crime, terror, war, abduction or October 7; relatives known only as someone's son, daughter or spouse; adult-film performers.
- A person whose name starts with a longer deck name (בת שבע, בת חן) stays with that name, not with בת.
- Every pick was then read by hand. 46 more people were removed for being political, controversial or a poor fit; they are listed with reasons in `people-exclude.json`.
- The 10 hand-written sample cards now use Wikidata people too, so every card follows the same rule.
