# Design System: Nest · בחירת שם

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Warm and quiet" (BRAND.md: "The palette is warm and quiet")**

The name swiper is Nest's brand applied to one task: deciding on names, one card at a time. Most of every screen is near-black `ink` text on a soft warm-grey `canvas` or on one of three warm, quiet photos (fabric, a notebook, a nursery). Content sits on **liquid glass** panels over the photos and on white `surface` cards with `shadow-hero` when the background is off ("ללא רקע"). Warmth is used sparingly: `peach` and `peach-soft` for blobs, the sex tag and the "אהבתי מאוד" button; `clay` for the logo's egg dot, a small dot badge and the year chart's 2024 dot.

The voice of the visuals matches the copy: a calm, organized friend. Generous radii, one family (Heebo) in three weights, few colors, one primary action in ink per screen. Matches are warm, never green, never confetti. Nothing animates for decoration.

Hebrew, right to left, throughout. Layout uses logical sides (start/end); only directional icons mirror.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Ink on canvas, with peach and clay as rare accents.
- Liquid glass over photos; solid white cards without a photo, under reduced transparency, and where `backdrop-filter` is missing.
- One type family (Heebo 500/700/800) with a large, named scale for the name itself.
- Pills for every button and chip; `radius-xl` for the name card.
- Short, calm motion; reduced motion turns movement into fades.

## Colors

A warm neutral palette with two brand warms; status colors exist in the tokens but the swiper rarely needs them.

### Primary
- **Ink** (`ink`): primary text, the wordmark, primary buttons ("שמירה לרשימה", the apply buttons), selected chips, the toast, the "השמות שלי" pill, focus outlines. tokens.json: "15.2:1 on canvas."

### Secondary
- **Peach** (`peach`): the blurred blobs behind content on "ללא רקע" and on the duel; girls' share in the boys/girls bar. tokens.json: "Brand warmth: … blobs, highlight fills."
- **Peach soft** (`peach-soft`): the "אהבתי מאוד" pill, the sex tag behind `clay-deep` text, text selection. tokens.json: "App-icon background, empty-state gradients."
- **Peach pale** (`peach-pale`): very light warm banners on canvas.

### Tertiary
- **Clay** (`clay`): the egg dot in the logo, the small "active filter" dot on the filter button, the 2024 dot on the sparkline and year chart. tokens.json: "Not for text: 3.0:1 on canvas."
- **Clay deep** (`clay-deep`): accent text: the sex tag, the love button's label, the love stamp. tokens.json: "5.7:1 on canvas, 5.1:1 on peach-soft."

### Neutral
- **Ink soft** (`ink-soft`): secondary text and section labels on canvas, surface and glass. tokens.json: "8.8:1 on canvas."
- **Stone** (`stone`): supporting text (subtitles, metadata, placeholders in inputs). tokens.json: "5.1:1 on canvas, 5.7:1 on surface."
- **Mist** (`mist`): icons and placeholders only, "never body copy (3.2:1 on canvas)".
- **Faint** (`faint`): disabled states and hairlines, "Not for meaningful text."
- **Canvas** (`canvas`): the app background behind everything; also the wells inside solid cards.
- **Surface** (`surface`): cards, sheets and inputs on canvas; solid glass.
- **Surface dim** (`surface-dim`): the nearer peeking card under the current one on "ללא רקע".
- **Sand** (`sand`): quiet fills: unselected chips on "ללא רקע", the farther peeking card, the "התאמה" badge behind ink text.
- **Line** (`line`): 1 px dividers, borders of soft glass on "ללא רקע".

### Status (administrative only)
- `info` / `info-bg`, `caution` / `caution-bg`, `urgent` / `urgent-bg`. In the swiper, `urgent` marks an invalid input border (add-name). BRAND.md: they "describe administrative state only … never imply a medical result", and never by color alone.

### Overlay tokens
White and ink at fixed opacities for glass and overlays, from the token script: `white-30`, `white-55`, `white-60`, `white-70`, `white-75`, `peach-pale-60`, `ink-35`, `ink-45`, `ink-75`.

### Named Rules
**The Ink Primary Rule.** Primary buttons are `ink` with white text; selected chips are `ink`. BRAND.md: "Avoid colored primary buttons."

**The Never Green Rule.** A match, a "done" or a chosen name is never shown in green and never celebrated with confetti (tasks/prd-names-pwa.md FR-W20, tasks/prd-name-detail.md §3.2.13).

**The Clay Is Not Text Rule.** `clay` fills dots and small accents; text in a warm accent uses `clay-deep`.

**The Text Never Turns White Rule.** Over any photo, including the user's own, text stays ink on glass; legibility comes from stronger glass and a scrim, not white text (tasks/prd-names-pwa.md §3.2.15).

## Typography

**Font:** Heebo (with `system-ui`, "Noto Sans Hebrew", Arial, sans-serif), self-hosted as a variable face covering weights 500–800, Hebrew and Latin subsets (`font-nest`).

**Character:** one rounded, sturdy sans for everything. Headings are ExtraBold (800), card titles Bold (700), running text Medium (500) (BRAND.md).

### Hierarchy
Each token sets size, line height and weight together (Tailwind `text-<name>`).

- **Name** (`text-name`): the name on the swipe card and the detail sheet header, with `-1px` tracking.
- **Match name** (`text-match-name`), **Duel name** (`text-duel-name`), **Hero name** (`text-hero-name`, the intro's small card illustration): the name at its moment sizes.
- **Display** (`text-display`): photo hero headline. `text-display-xl` is the landing hero only and isn't used in `/names`.
- **Title** (`text-title`): screen titles ("בחירת שם", "השמות שלי"), sheet titles.
- **Section** (`text-section`) and **Row name** (`text-row-name`): section headings inside a screen; the name in a list row.
- **Card title** (`text-card-title`): card and list-item titles, the surname line, the text-link button.
- **Button** (`text-button`): large button labels. **Intro** (`text-intro`): the card's intro line, the decision pills, inputs.
- **Body** (`text-body`): running text in cards and sheets, the toast, the base size of `.nest-names`. **Quote** (`text-quote`), **Ref** (`text-ref`), **Person** (`text-person`): the verse, its reference and a known-person row; `text-person` also sets the "השמות שלי" pill.
- **Note** (`text-note`), **Match line** (`text-match-line`), **Stat** (`text-stat`, the number in a stat tile), **Stamp** (`text-stamp`, the swipe stamps), **Peek** (`text-peek`, the name on the farther peeking card).
- **Caption** (`text-caption`): metadata, chip labels, the card's position line.
- **Tag** (`text-tag`) and **Label** (`text-label`): the sex tag, section labels in the detail sheet, small badges.

### Named Rules
**The Token-Only Type Rule.** No literal font size anywhere in names code; `tests/names/designGuard.test.ts` fails the build on one. A genuinely new style is a new token in the token script.

**The Own Direction Rule.** Numbers, years and Latin words inside Hebrew lines keep their own direction (`<bdi dir="ltr">`); charts draw left to right on the SVG only.

## Layout

A single column for a phone. The screen side gutter is `space-5` (`px-5`), narrowing to `px-3` under 22.5rem; card padding is `space-5`/`space-4`; gaps between card sections follow `space-3` (`gap-3`); chips and small controls sit `space-2` apart. Content is capped at `max-w-md` and centered on wider screens.

The swipe screen is a fixed stack, top to bottom: header (title, background, filter and settings buttons, the "השמות שלי" pill), quick chips, the card stack (current card plus two peeking cards, −3° and +2°), the action bar (three equal decision pills and undo), and the status footer. The card never scrolls at 390 × 844; it is slim and points to the detail sheet.

Sheets rise from the bottom over a scrim, with a handle, a title row (or a custom header), scrolling content and an optional pinned footer above the bottom safe area. The name sheet opens at `min(70dvh, content)` and drags to full height (`100dvh` minus the top safe area). From 768 px wide, sheets are a centered panel (max 480 px, up to 90vh) without detents.

Safe areas are respected at the top (full-height sheets) and bottom (sheet footers, the toast). Logical properties only: `ms-/me-/ps-/pe-/start-/end-`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from glass over a photo, or from soft shadows on white when there is no photo. Shadows are low and warm (ink-tinted), never hard.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`shadow-card`): white cards on canvas; the farther peeking card on "ללא רקע".
- **Card raised** (`shadow-card-raised`): the nearer peeking card on "ללא רקע".
- **Hero** (`shadow-hero`): solid glass panels (on "ללא רקע", reduced transparency, no `backdrop-filter`).
- **Glass** (`shadow-glass`): liquid glass panels over photos, with the inset top highlight.
- **Button** (`shadow-button`): the ink primary pill and the toast; **Button soft** (`shadow-button-soft`) for the same on "ללא רקע".

### Glass panels
- **Strong glass** (`names-glass`, `Glass` default): `glass-tint` fill, `glass-filter` blur and saturation, `glass-border`, `shadow-glass`. For sheets, the sign-in panel, menus, the secondary pill.
- **Card glass** (`names-glass names-card-glass`): the name card and other reading panels (sign-in, intro); the whiter `glass-well-tint` fill and less saturation, so the photo reads as warm light behind a white card.
- **Soft glass** (`names-glass-soft`, `Glass variant="soft"`): `glass-soft-tint`, `glass-soft-filter`, `glass-soft-border`. For round icon buttons, unselected chips, hint chips.
- **Well** (`names-well`): a tile inside a glass card (the stat tiles), `glass-well-tint` and `glass-well-border`.
- **Own photo** (`data-names-bg="own"`): stronger fills `glass-own-tint`, `glass-own-soft-tint`, `glass-own-well-tint`, plus a canvas-colored scrim whose strength follows the luminance under the card.

### Backgrounds
Five choices (sheet "רקע למסך"): "ללא רקע" (canvas with `bg-none` peach gradients and blurred `peach` blobs at `blob-blur`), "בדים" (default), "מחברת", "חדר תינוק", and "תמונה משלי" (a resized photo kept on the device). Photo screens carry the top scrim `scrim-photo` at `scrim-photo-height` so the header reads.

### Named Rules
**The Solid Fallback Rule.** Glass turns solid `surface` with `shadow-hero` on "ללא רקע", under `prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce`, and where `backdrop-filter` isn't supported. Wells become `canvas`.

## Shapes

Soft and generous. Pills (`radius-pill`) for every button, chip, tag, the toast and the "השמות שלי" pill; `radius-xl` for the name card and empty-state cards; `radius-lg` for cards and glass cards; `radius-md` for inputs, menus and quick tiles; `radius-sm` for icon wells and small tiles. Borders are 1 px (glass borders or `line`). Line icons have a 2 px stroke with round caps and joins, Feather/Lucide style, the same family as the logo's line.

## Components

All in `src/components/names/ui/` unless noted. Fix a pattern in the shared component, never per screen.

### Buttons (`Button`, `IconButton`, `buttonClass`, `iconButtonClass` in `ui/classes.ts`)
- **Primary** (`variant="primary"`): ink pill, white text; `lg` is 56 px tall with `text-button` and `shadow-button`, `md` is 44 px with bold `text-body`. One per screen or sheet: the main action.
- **Secondary** (`variant="secondary"`): strong-glass pill with ink text ("לכל הפרטים", "דילוג"-style quiet actions); solid surface with a `line` border on "ללא רקע".
- **Peach** (`variant="peach"`): a round `peach-soft` button with `clay-deep` icon; needs a Hebrew `aria-label`.
- **Link** (`variant="link"`): underlined ink text, `text-card-title`, at least 44 px tall.
- **IconButton**: 44 px round soft-glass button with an icon only; the Hebrew `aria-label` is required by its type. For close ("סגירה"), filters, background, settings, row menus.
- **Row menu** (`lists/RowMenu`): strong-glass `radius-md` menu anchored to its button's end. It opens below the button, or above it when it wouldn't fit below the screen (less the home-bar inset) and there's more room above. Width from content (`w-max`), at most the page's content width; a long label (the choose item carries the name) wraps inside it, items stay ≥ 44 px. A name the user added gets no "לא להציג יותר" (it was never in the deck).
- **Pressed / disabled:** press scales to 0.95 in 150 ms (`motion-safe` only); disabled is 60% opacity. `touch-manipulation` on all.
- **Controls aren't text:** one base rule in `names.css` gives every `button`, control role (`button`, `tab`, `switch`, `radio`, `menuitem`), checkbox/radio label and `a[data-control]` no text selection, no iOS long-press callout or drag, `touch-action: manipulation`, and the pointer cursor (not when disabled). A link that looks like a button or row carries `data-control` (`Button`/`IconButton` with `href` add it); inline text links stay content and keep their callout.
- **No pull-to-refresh:** `html` and `body` get `overscroll-behavior-y: none` while a `/names` screen is mounted (`:has(.nest-names)`, so the landing keeps it). A pull past the top doesn't drag the screen down or reload it; inner scrollers (sheets, the card) use `overscroll-contain`.

### Decision buttons (`src/components/names/DecisionButtons.tsx`)
Three equal pills, each with its word and icon, shared by the swipe screen's `ActionBar` and the detail sheet: "שמירה לרשימה" (ink, primary), "אהבתי מאוד" (peach-soft with `clay-deep`), "דילוג" (quiet white glass). In the sheet, the current vote shows as pressed with an ink ring and a check in place of the icon, so it isn't told by color.

### Chips (`Chip`, `chipClass`, `hintChipClass`)
- **Toggle chip:** selected is ink with white text; unselected is soft glass with ink text (`sand` with `ink-soft` on "ללא רקע"); state via `aria-pressed`. A 29 px pill whose hit area stretches to 45 px.
- **Hint chip:** the unselected look, not a control (the card's "מהתנ״ך" / "{n} אנשים מוכרים").

### Switch (settings opt-in)
A 52 × 32 px pill track with a 2 px edge: off is `sand` with a `stone` edge (≥ 3:1 against `surface`), on is `ink`. The 24 px `surface` thumb sits at the start and slides 20 px with `translate` (150 ms ease-out, mirrored in RTL); the track color eases with it. The state is also said in words under the title ("פעיל" / "כבוי").

### Glass panels (`Glass`, `glassClass`)
See Elevation & Depth. `Glass` renders the panel as `div`, `section`, `article`, `header`, `nav` or `aside`.

### Bottom sheet (`BottomSheet`, `ui/detents.ts`, `ui/layers.ts`, `ui/useModal.ts`)
Every sheet. Bound to `?sheet=<name>` (open with `openSheet`, so Back closes it); modal dialog semantics, focus trap and return, scrim, handle, title row or custom header, optional `headerEnd` and pinned `footer`. Opt-in tall sheet with `detents={["70", "full"]}` (the name sheet): a handle drag of `DETENT_DRAG` (96 px) or more changes height or closes. Only one layer at a time.

### Dialog (`Dialog`)
Confirm dialogs (`role="alertdialog"`) bound to `?dialog=<name>`; Escape, Back and the scrim cancel; the safe action ("ביטול") takes focus.

### Toast (`ToastRegion`, `src/lib/names/toast.ts`)
The one toast: an ink pill near the bottom with `text-body` bold white text, an optional action ("ביטול"), `shadow-button`. Its `role="status"` region is always mounted, so screen readers hear every message; over a sheet with pinned buttons it sits just above them.
Reversible acts that leave no row to look at get the toast's undo instead of a confirm dialog: restoring a hidden name ("השם {name} יחזור לבחירה") and taking the chosen name back ("הבחירה בשם {name} בוטלה", then "השם {name} נבחר שוב"). The act's label says what it undoes ("ביטול הבחירה"), so "ביטול" on the toast reads as undo.

### Legal line (`auth/LegalLine.tsx`)
The line under the welcome and join panels ("בהמשך מאשרים את תנאי השימוש ומדיניות הפרטיות"): one element for both screens, a `names-glass` (strong) pill with `radius-lg`, `text-caption` `ink` text and bold underlined `ink` links, ≥ 4.5:1 over the photo's darkest fold. Each link owns a 44 px tall tap area (`-my-3 inline-flex min-h-11`) without growing the pill, and the "ו" stays with "מדיניות הפרטיות" when the line wraps at 320 px.

### Panel notes (`auth/PanelNote.tsx`)
A notice inside the auth glass panel (offline, sign-ups closed, session expired) is a line, not a box: a 20 px drawn icon (offline, info, clock) and `text-body` medium `ink` text, ≥ 7.8:1 on the glass. It sits in the panel's `gap-6` above the buttons inside an always-mounted `role="status"` region (`display: contents`, so an empty one adds no gap).

Errors on the auth glass (welcome's Google failure, the sign-in email and code errors, onboarding's save error) are the same line with the `alert` icon (a circled "!") in **bold** `ink`, `role="alert"`, never `urgent` red: red falls under 4.5:1 where the glass crosses the photo, and the icon plus the weight say "error" without color. Caption-size errors inside a hint (onboarding's name error and its missing-fields line once a submit was tried) use `ErrorMark` (the same icon at 16 px, inline) and `CAPTION_ERROR` (`font-bold text-ink`). On opaque surfaces (sheets, settings) errors stay `font-bold text-urgent`.

### Name card (`src/components/names/card/`)
`NameCard` on `names-glass names-card-glass`, `radius-xl`, padding `space-5`: `CardHeader` (sex tag in `peach-soft`/`clay-deep`, position line, the name in `text-name`, niqqud, surname line), the intro clamped to two lines, hint chips, `CardSexBar` (boys `ink`, girls `peach`), `CardStats` (two well tiles with `text-stat` and a sparkline), and "לכל הפרטים" (secondary `md`). Swipe stamps show while dragging: keep in `ink`, skip in `ink-soft`, love in `clay-deep`. `CardStack` adds the two peeking cards (`names-back-near`, `names-back-far`). On a photo they sit in `names-back-clip`, masked to the strip under the current card and its two bottom corners, so their edges never show through the card's glass; while the card moves (`data-moving` from `useSwipeDrag`) the mask lifts and the next card shows whole.

### Detail sheet (`src/components/names/detail/`)
The tall `BottomSheet` with sections in board order (surname, meaning, together, numbers with `YearChart`, boys/girls, facts, similar, source), each through `Section`: a `text-tag` label in `ink-soft` and a `gap-2` body. The together badge is ink on `sand` with a small heart. Decision buttons pinned in the footer.

### Inputs
Every text field uses `inputClass()` (`ui/classes.ts`): `surface`, `radius-md`, 48 px tall, `text-intro` ink with `stone` placeholders, and a 1 px edge that reads at ≥ 3:1: `stone` on solid surfaces (sheets, cards), `ink-soft` on glass, where the photo behind can be darker. Read-only fields (the invite link) are `sand`. Invalid turns the edge `urgent` (`aria-invalid`). Focus is the global 2 px ink outline drawn on the field's own edge (`-outline-offset-1`, no floating second ring); a focused invalid field keeps an `urgent` outline. Kinds: `code` (the sign-in code's centered `text-title` digits), `multiline` (textareas; no resize grip on touch) and `link` (the read-only invite URL in `text-caption`, so it fits).

### Empty and end panels (`emptyPanelClass`, `emptyTitleClass`)
Every empty or end state uses `emptyPanelClass(surface)` (`ui/classes.ts`): a centered column, `gap-3`, `px-5 py-6`, `radius-xl`. `"glass"` is the panel where the swipe card was (deck end, empty filter, the invite and recap pauses, the deck's load error): it fills the card's room and scrolls inside itself when its content is taller. `"card"` is the white `shadow-card` card of an empty list ("שמרתי", "התאמות", the solo invite on "התאמות", "שמות שהסתרתי", the duel with too few names). The title follows the surface (`emptyTitleClass`): `text-title` bold ink on the deck's panels, `text-card-title` ink on a list's card; secondary lines are `ink-soft` (`emptyLineClass`). Titles and lines are balanced (`text-balance`). A list's empty card is an `h2` title, one line on how the list fills ("שמות ששומרים או אוהבים בכרטיסים יופיעו כאן."), then the way to fill it ("לבחירת שמות"; the duel with one saved name adds "הוספת שם" beside it); never a bare "nothing here".

When the votes couldn't be read on a device with no cache (`loadFailed` in the vote store, retried every 30 s), an empty list would be a lie: "שמרתי" and "שמות שהסתרתי" show `lists/VotesLoadFailed` instead (the same card, "לא הצלחנו לטעון את הרשימה." + "מנסים שוב מעצמנו בעוד רגע." + "ניסיון חוזר", title and line in a `contents` status region), the "שמרתי" tab and the deck's "השמות שלי" pill drop their count, and the deck's sync line says "לא הצלחנו לטעון את הבחירות. מנסים שוב." The deck stays usable (offline-first).

### Checkboxes (`checkboxClass`)
Onboarding's three consents and the filter sheet's "מספר אותיות מותאם אישית" use `checkboxClass()` (`ui/classes.ts`): a drawn 24 px box (`appearance: none`, half of `radius-sm`, so it never reads as a radio), `surface` fill and a 2 px edge at ≥ 3:1 like the fields: `stone` on solid surfaces, `ink-soft` on glass. Checked is an `ink` box with a `surface` tick (`names.css` `.names-checkbox`), so the state is told by the mark, not the color. Invalid and unchecked turns the edge `urgent`. The whole label row is the ≥ 44 px target; focus is the global 2 px ink outline 2 px off the box (on the ink box an outline on its edge would vanish).

### Navigation
No tab bar. The swipe header's "השמות שלי" ink pill and the settings button lead to lists and settings; other screens have a `BackButton` and a title. Lists switches between "שמרתי", "התאמות" and "דו־קרב" with segments in the URL (`?tab=`).

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use only token names: Tailwind `bg-ink`, `text-stone`, `rounded-lg`, `shadow-card`, `text-title`, `font-nest`, and the `names-*` glass classes. A new value is a new token (`tokens.json` or the token script, then `npm run tokens:css`).
- **Do** keep contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for text on canvas, glass and every photo; `mist` and `faint` are never readable text.
- **Do** give every icon-only control a Hebrew `aria-label` and a 44 × 44 px target.
- **Do** carry status with a word and an icon as well as color.
- **Do** keep a `prefers-reduced-motion` version of every animation (`names.css` caps everything else at near zero; short fades marked `data-motion="fade"` keep 200 ms).
- **Do** use logical properties and mirror only directional icons; never mirror the logo.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use hex, rgb or px font sizes in names code (`tests/names/designGuard.test.ts`).
- **Don't** use colored primary buttons, green for matches or success, or confetti.
- **Don't** set text in `clay`, or switch text to white over a photo.
- **Don't** rebuild the wordmark in Heebo, add a container behind it, or recolor the dot.
- **Don't** make the swipe card scroll on a 390 × 844 phone.
- **Don't** add a UI library or animation dependency; the system is CSS, tokens and these components.

### Motion as it is today
Quoted from `src/app/names/names.css`, `ui/classes.ts` and `swipe/useSwipeDrag.ts`. The curves and durations are motion tokens (`motion` in `tokens.json` → `--nest-ease-*` / `--nest-duration-*` in `nest-tokens.css`, and `src/styles/nest-motion.ts` for Web Animations calls): `ease-out` `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`, `ease-drawer` `cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` (not used yet), `duration-press` 150, `-fade` 160, `-fast` 200, `-snap` 220, `-sheet` 260, `-card` 280, `-fling` 300, `-fling-quick` 180 ms. Use a token, never an inlined value; in classes `duration-(--nest-duration-press)`.
- Sheet in: `names-sheet-in`, 260 ms, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`. Sheet out: `names-sheet-out`, 200 ms ease-in. Scrim and toast: `names-fade-in` 200 ms ease-out; scrim out `names-fade-out` 200 ms ease-in. Dialog in: `names-dialog-in` 200 ms ease-out (also the desktop sheet panel).
- Card enter: `names-card-in` (from 14 px lower, 0.96 scale, half opacity), 280 ms ease-out.
- Card fling (Web Animations API): the buttons and keys fly the card off in 300 ms `ease-out`. A released drag hands its speed on (`flingTiming` in `lib/names/gesture.ts`, speed read from the moves of the last 80 ms): the same curve with its start bent to the finger's speed (never slower than an even pace), shortened toward 180 ms only for a flick too fast for 300 ms, so there's no stall between dragging and flying. A drag released while moving back toward the middle faster than 0.2 px/ms snaps back, however far out it is. Snap back: 220 ms `ease-out`. The decision is made as the fling starts: a fixed, inert copy of the card flies off while the next card enters under it, so taps, keys and "ביטול" during the fling are never dropped (undo lands the copy at once).
- Press: scale 0.95 in 150 ms, `motion-safe` only.
- Reduced motion: sheets and scrim fade in 160 ms; a decision is a 160 ms fade instead of a fling; the next card fades in (`data-motion="fade"`, capped at 200 ms); everything else is effectively off.
- BRAND.md: the logo animation (2.8 s) plays once only, and reduced motion shows the static mark.
