# A11Y · G2: Deck (swipe screen, name sheet, filters, background, about, report, survey, match and decision sheets, pauses, deck end)

Run 2026-11-07 by US-A-A11Y-G2 at `60525c0` (no app code change since the baselines).

Skills read: `accessibility-audit` (`SKILL.md`; `references/wcag-checklist.md`, `references/common-issues-and-fixes.md` (section list; §1, §11, §12 for the entries below), `references/testing-tools-and-techniques.md` was read for G1 and its method is unchanged here), `accessibility-review` (`SKILL.md`, WCAG quick reference and output format). Method: accessibility-audit's 5-layer process on every G2 state in `scores.md` "Screens G2" (40 of 45: see "Not probed" below):
1. **Automated:** axe-core (`mobile.mjs` `axe(page)`, WCAG 2.0–2.2 A/AA + best practice) on all 40 states in WebKit iPhone 15 and Chromium Pixel 7, on the dev server, with every backend call mocked as in `us006-g2.mjs`.
2. **Keyboard:** the deck flow (focus the card, K / S / Z, Enter opens the name sheet, Tab ×40 inside it, Escape, Shift+F10 on "דילוג", the header buttons open their sheets and get focus back), the Tab order of every state from its start, focus visibility, whether anything covers the focused element, and where focus lands after a decision, at the deck end, on a pause and on a similar name. WebKit tabs only to form fields (Safari's default), so the full order comes from Chromium.
3. **Screen reader:** the accessibility tree of every state (Playwright `ariaSnapshot`, `*.aria.txt`) against the skill's "What to Listen For" checklist; the text of every live region after each step (decision, undo, offline, sheet vote, chip in filters, pause, deck end).
4. **Visual:** contrast of every text node in both engines (pixels under each text box in a shot with all text transparent, one viewport shot per scroll position; foreground = computed colour with alpha and ancestor opacity; reported at the median pixel; text under `inert` is skipped, since it sits behind the scrim). Then the field edges against their surface (1.4.11), targets under 44 / 24 px, and 1.4.12 text spacing on the six densest states (deck, name sheet, filters, round recap, match, decision). Playwright WebKit draws the glass too white (no `backdrop-filter: saturate()`), so the deciding numbers for glass come from Chromium; the Simulator shots from US-006 (same code) were checked by eye.
5. **Flow:** decide, undo, offline with a pending decision, vote on a similar name from the sheet, filter to an empty result, reach the deck end (solo, shared, nothing saved), the 5th-save invite pause, the 30-decision recap, the survey prompt, a new match, the chosen-name sheet.

Assumptions and skips: as in G1, no Figma (`accessibility-review`'s input and `CONNECTORS.md` don't exist here) and no browser extensions: axe-core through Playwright does the same and needs no new dependency (PRD §3.1.7). No real VoiceOver pass: VoiceOver can't be driven in the Simulator with `cliclick`; the accessibility tree and the live-region texts stand in for it. A real-device VoiceOver pass before launch is still worth it, mainly for the swipe card and the sheets. Severity: the skill's Critical/High → P1, Moderate (AA) → P2, Low/best practice → P3. Problems earlier lenses already filed are listed at the end, not filed again.

**Not probed (with reasons):** `deck-bg-own` and `detail-70-bg-own` (the own-photo upload fails in Playwright WebKit, and the photo is the person's own; checked by eye in `deck-bg-own-sim.png`: every text sits on the card's glass and reads), `background-own-saved` (same upload), `deck-drag-start` (the mirror of `deck-drag-end`, which was probed), `detail-full-bg-none` (the full detent was probed on fabric; "ללא רקע" changes only the page behind the sheet).

### What passes (checked)

- **Structure (1.3.1, 2.4.2, 2.4.6, 3.1.1):** `lang="he"`, `dir="rtl"`, title "בחירת שם"; `header` + `main` landmarks; one `h1` ("בחירת שם"), the card's name an `h2`, each sheet's title an `h2` with `h3` sections (no skipped levels); the end and pause panels are `section`s labelled by their `h2`; the Nest card is an `aside` with its own label; the saved names on the recap and the deck end are real lists.
- **Name, role, value (4.1.2):** every control in all 40 states has a name (probe: 0 unnamed); the action buttons' names contain their visible words ("שמירה לרשימה" / "שמירה", "אהבתי מאוד" / "אהבתי", 2.5.3); the vote state in the sheet is `aria-pressed`; the filter chips are `aria-pressed` toggles; "סינון, פעיל" says the filter state in words, not only by the dot (1.4.1); the background picker is a `radiogroup` with a roving tabindex and arrow keys; the skip button is `aria-haspopup="menu"` with `aria-expanded`; "ביטול" is `aria-disabled` (still focusable) with nothing to undo; the saved count is in the link's name ("השמות שלי: 0 נשמרו").
- **Text alternatives (1.1.1):** the sex bar and both year charts are `role=img` with full sentences; the sheet's chart also has a real `table` with every year (caption, `th` row and column headers); the back cards, swipe stamps and decorative dots are `aria-hidden`; every icon `svg` sits inside a labelled control.
- **Dialogs (keyboard pattern in the checklist §11):** every sheet is `role=dialog` `aria-modal="true"` with a name (the name, "סינון", "רקע למסך", "על הנתונים", "דיווח על טעות בשם …", "שאלון קצר", "יש התאמה: נועם", "בחרתי שם: נועם"); the page behind is `inert`; focus moves in on open (the title or the panel), Tab wraps inside (40 Tabs in the name sheet cycle its 12 stops), Escape closes, and focus goes back to the opener (card → sheet → card; "בחירת רקע" and "סינון" → sheet → same button).
- **Keyboard (2.1.1, 2.1.2, 2.1.4, 2.4.3, 2.4.7):** the whole deck works without a pointer: Tab reaches settings, background, filters, my names, the card, "על הנתונים", "לכל הפרטים", the three decisions and undo in visual RTL order; K / L / S and Z work only while focus is in the swipe area, so they can't fire by accident from elsewhere (2.1.4 "active only on focus"), and they're described to screen readers by `aria-describedby`; Enter on the card opens the sheet; Shift+F10 (or the menu key) on "דילוג" opens the hide menu with focus on its item, and Escape returns focus to "דילוג". Every focused control shows the 2 px ink outline; nothing focused is covered (`elementFromPoint`).
- **Focus after actions:** a decision by key keeps focus on the card, which now shows the next name; by button, focus stays on the button; the deck end moves focus to its first link; a pause moves focus to its main button (see -03 for what is lost there); a similar name moves focus to the new title.
- **Status messages (4.1.3):** decisions are announced from an always-mounted `role=status` ("השם זלמן נשמר. השם הבא: איילת", "דילוג על איילת…", "בוטל: איילת"); the offline line has its own always-mounted region ("אין חיבור · בחירה אחת ממתינה לסנכרון", with an icon, not colour alone); a vote on a similar name inside the sheet is announced by the toast region, which stays outside the inert page; the filters sheet announces the live count ("518 שמות מתאימים").
- **Pointer (2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.7, 2.5.8):** every swipe has a button (and a key); the long-press menu also opens by right click, the menu key or Shift+F10; decisions fire on click, not on pointerdown. Every target is ≥ 44 px or reaches it through its `::before` hit area ("על הנתונים", the chips); the only exceptions are already filed (IMP-C-G2-01, PRO-G2-01).
- **Contrast (1.4.3), measured:** on the deck card, the name sheet and every sheet on white, all body, caption and label text is ≥ 4.5:1 in both engines (the low readings in the raw output are text scrolled under the sheets' pinned buttons, the screen-reader-only table and the transient swipe stamp, checked one by one). The sync pill, the toast and the Nest card on the end and pause panels pass. Focus outline ≥ 3:1 on glass, photo, white and against the ink buttons (it's drawn 2 px outside).
- **Reflow and spacing (1.4.10, 1.4.12):** no horizontal overflow at 320 px (baseline `webkit320` shots, IMP-A-G2) or with the 1.4.12 spacing; no clipped text with the spacing on the six densest states (`spacing-*-chromium.png`).

## Findings

### A11Y-G2-01: "יש התאמה" and "בחרתי שם" are 2.3–3.1:1 on the photo

- **Screen and state:** match sheet (new match), decision sheet ("בחרתי שם" solo, "בחרנו שם" shared, over `/names/lists`)
- **Browser:** chromium-pixel (deciding) · webkit-iphone
- **What's wrong:** both full-screen sheets open with a small bold eyebrow in `text-clay-deep` (#8A5225, 13 px bold) that sits on the bare background photo, above the glass name card. Over the fabric photo it measures 2.3:1 ("יש התאמה", Chromium; 2.3:1 WebKit) and 3.1:1 ("בחרתי שם" / "בחרנו שם", Chromium; 2.8:1 WebKit). That line is what the moment is: the match or the choice. A screen reader gets it from the dialog's name, but someone with low vision reads a faint brown word on brown cloth. On the same sheets the births line under the name (`text-caption text-stone`, 12 px) is 3.6:1 on the decision card and 4.1:1 on the match card in Chromium (3.9–4.2:1 WebKit), where the glass lets the photo through, and on the decision sheet the Nest card's "מה זה Nest?" link and wordmark line read 4.1:1 at the card's bottom edge, which crosses the photo's darkest fold.
- **Rule it breaks:** `accessibility-audit` · `references/wcag-checklist.md` §3 Text Contrast (1.4.3 AA) · "Normal text: ≥ 4.5:1. Large text (18pt+ or 14pt+ bold): ≥ 3:1" (13 px bold is normal text); `references/common-issues-and-fixes.md` §1 "Low Text Contrast"; `accessibility-review` · `SKILL.md` · "1.4.3 Contrast ratio >= 4.5:1 (normal text)"
- **Severity:** P2 (AA failure on the title of the two biggest moments; the dialog name still carries it for screen readers)
- **Effort:** S
- **Proposed change:** in `MatchSheet.tsx:126` and `DecisionSheet.tsx:70` make the eyebrow `text-ink` (ink on the light upper photo ≈ 9:1+) and keep the clay dot under the name as the one accent, so the sheets stay calm and never green (PRD §3.1.3). Births line (`MatchSheet.tsx:135`, `DecisionSheet.tsx:79`): `text-ink-soft` instead of `text-stone` (ink-soft is what the deck card uses on the same glass, 5.1:1 or more). Nest card on the decision sheet: give `NestCard` the strong `names-glass` surface instead of `variant="soft"` only where it sits on the bare photo (decision sheet), or move the decision sheet's content up so the card doesn't cross the dark fold (IMP-C-G2-08 moves the main action up anyway). Re-measure in Chromium and the Simulator (≥ 4.5:1 at the median). Check with ELEV-G2 (it lists the clay-deep eyebrow as part of the match/decision structure): the eyebrow keeps its size and weight, only its colour changes.
- **Guardrail:** polish (existing tokens, no token value changes; same copy and layout)
- **Screenshot:** `audit/A11Y-G2/match-default-chromium.png`, `audit/A11Y-G2/decision-solo-chromium.png`, `audit/A11Y-G2/decision-shared-webkit.png`

### A11Y-G2-02: The report and survey fields have no visible edge on the white sheet

- **Screen and state:** report sheet (`?sheet=report`, "מה לא נכון?"), survey sheet (`?sheet=survey`, the two open questions)
- **Browser:** webkit-iphone · chromium-pixel · sim-safari (same in all)
- **What's wrong:** the three textareas are `bg-surface` (white) with a `border-line` (#E6DDD6) edge on a white sheet. The edge is 1.34:1 against the sheet and the field itself 1.0:1, so the only cue that there's a box to type in is a hairline that disappears on many phones (`survey-default-sim.png`: the "למי לדעתך זה הכי מתאים?" field is barely there). The survey's radio rows are fine: the native radio circle inside each row marks it. This is the same problem as A11Y-G1-01 on the sign-in and onboarding fields.
- **Rule it breaks:** `accessibility-audit` · `references/wcag-checklist.md` §3 Non-Text Contrast (1.4.11 AA) · "UI components (buttons, inputs, dropdowns): ≥ 3:1 against adjacent colors"; `accessibility-review` · `SKILL.md` · "1.4.11 Non-text contrast >= 3:1 (UI components, graphics)"
- **Severity:** P2
- **Effort:** XS (once A11Y-G1-01's shared field class exists)
- **Proposed change:** merge with A11Y-G1-01: use its shared text-field class (`border-stone`, 5.7:1 on white) for `field` in `SurveySheet.tsx:34` and the textarea in `about/ReportSheet.tsx:158`. In the same pass ELEV-G2-05 (resize grip, focus ring on the field's edge) and IMP-A-G2-04 (16 px) touch these two fields; do them together.
- **Guardrail:** polish (existing token)
- **Screenshot:** `audit/A11Y-G2/survey-default-sim.png`, `audit/A11Y-G2/report-default-chromium.png`, `audit/A11Y-G2/survey-default-chromium.png`

### A11Y-G2-03: On a pause, a screen reader hears the next name and skips the panel's text

- **Screen and state:** `/names` invite pause (after the 5th save) and round recap (after 30 decisions)
- **Browser:** webkit-iphone · chromium-pixel (same tree and live regions)
- **What's wrong:** two things happen at once when the decision that triggers a pause is made. (1) The decision status still says what it says on every vote, "השם זלמן נשמר. השם הבא: איילת", but איילת isn't on screen: the pause panel replaced the card, and the person has to press "אחר כך, להמשיך לבחור" first. (2) Focus moves to the panel's main button ("הזמנה לבחירה משותפת" / "להמשיך לבחור"), so a screen reader reads the region name (the `h2`) and the button, and skips the paragraph that explains the pause ("כבר שמרת 5 שמות. אפשר להזמין את מי שבוחר איתך…", "על מה דילגת נשאר רק אצלך.", or the recap line "עברת על 30 שמות ושמרת 8."). So the person hears that the next name is איילת, then an invite button with no reason given. (PRO-G2 saw the stale status and left it for this lens.)
- **Rule it breaks:** `accessibility-audit` · `references/wcag-checklist.md` §11 Status Messages (4.1.3 AA) · "Success messages announced via `aria-live="polite"` or `role="status"`" (the message announced is wrong for the state shown); §4 Focus Behavior · "Focus moves to new content when dynamically added"; `SKILL.md` Layer 3 · "Are status messages announced (success, error, loading)?"
- **Severity:** P2 (wrong information for screen-reader users at the two points where the app asks for something)
- **Effort:** S
- **Proposed change:** (1) in `swipe/SwipeScreen.tsx` don't append the next name when the decision opens a pause: `setNext(null)` when the pause condition is met (the pause is derived from the totals the decision just changed, so compute it before setting `next`), so the status is only "השם זלמן נשמר". (2) In `swipe/PausePanel.tsx`, give each panel's lead paragraph an id and point the autofocused button's `aria-describedby` at it (the invite pause: both paragraphs; the recap: the recap line). Same check for `EmptyDeck.tsx`: its `h2` already carries the message, so it needs nothing. Verify the live text and the description in both engines' `ariaSnapshot`.
- **Guardrail:** polish (no new copy; the existing lines are wired to the controls)
- **Screenshot:** `audit/A11Y-G2/deck-pause-invite-chromium.png`, `audit/A11Y-G2/deck-pause-invite-chromium.aria.txt`, `audit/A11Y-G2/deck-pause-recap-webkit.png`

### A11Y-G2-04: The name sheet has two "סגירה" buttons for screen readers

- **Screen and state:** name sheet (every detent), any sheet that has its own close button in the header (`closeInHeader`)
- **Browser:** webkit-iphone · chromium-pixel
- **What's wrong:** the drag handle is a `button aria-label="סגירה"`. When the sheet has a visible close button in its header, the handle leaves the Tab order (`tabIndex=-1`, NP-UX-010) but stays in the accessibility tree, so a screen reader swiping through the name sheet meets "סגירה, כפתור" twice before the name ("button "סגירה" / heading "זלמן" / … / button "סגירה"" in `detail-70-bg-fabric-chromium.aria.txt`). The handle's button is the right thing in the sheets without a header close (filters, report, survey, about, background), where it is the only close.
- **Rule it breaks:** `accessibility-audit` · `SKILL.md` Layer 3 · "Are buttons and links announced with their purpose?"; `references/wcag-checklist.md` §7 Links · "Links with identical text go to identical destinations" (the same idea for two controls with one name); best practice, not a WCAG failure
- **Severity:** P3
- **Effort:** XS
- **Proposed change:** in `ui/BottomSheet.tsx:280`, when `closeInHeader` is set, add `aria-hidden="true"` to the handle button as well as `tabIndex={-1}` (it keeps its pointer handlers for the drag; it's not focusable by Tab, so `aria-hidden` on it is allowed, and axe's `aria-hidden-focus` accepts `tabindex=-1`). Re-run axe and check the tree has one "סגירה".
- **Guardrail:** polish
- **Screenshot:** `audit/A11Y-G2/detail-70-bg-fabric-chromium.aria.txt`, `audit/A11Y-G2/detail-70-bg-fabric-chromium.png`

### A11Y-G2-05: The name sheet's source line becomes the page's "contentinfo" landmark

- **Screen and state:** name sheet, bottom of the content ("מקור: הלשכה המרכזית לסטטיסטיקה", "על הנתונים", "דיווח על טעות")
- **Browser:** webkit-iphone · chromium-pixel (both trees show `contentinfo`)
- **What's wrong:** `SourceSection` is a `<footer>`. The sheet is a `section role="dialog"` that portals into the page wrapper, and both engines expose this `footer` as a page-level `contentinfo` landmark instead of scoping it to the dialog (observed in both `ariaSnapshot`s; the deck itself has no footer). A screen-reader user who opens the landmarks list (VoiceOver's rotor) on the name sheet finds a "page footer" that is really the data source line of one name, and the page has no footer otherwise.
- **Rule it breaks:** `accessibility-audit` · `references/wcag-checklist.md` §2 Landmarks and Regions (1.3.1 A) · "Page uses semantic landmarks: `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<footer>`, `<aside>`" (used for what they mean); `SKILL.md` "The first rule of ARIA" / semantic HTML table
- **Severity:** P3 (best practice; nothing is hidden or wrong in the reading order)
- **Effort:** XS
- **Proposed change:** in `detail/SourceSection.tsx:16` use a `div` (or keep `footer` with `role="group"` and no label); the layout classes stay.
- **Guardrail:** polish
- **Screenshot:** `audit/A11Y-G2/detail-70-bg-fabric-webkit.aria.txt`
