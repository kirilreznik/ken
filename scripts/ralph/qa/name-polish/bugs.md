### NP-BUG-009: Offline after one online visit, the photo backgrounds don't load

- **Cycle found:** 1 · **QA story:** US-QA1-I · **Browser:** chromium-pixel (production build :3100, real service worker) · **Screen:** `/names` with the "בדים" background, and the background sheet, offline
- **Steps:** Fresh profile, QA user 1 with background "בדים". Open `/names` online once (the worker installs and precaches), go offline, reload `/names`, then tap "בחירת רקע". Probes: `shots/qa1i-bg.mjs chromium 1` (and `2` for two visits), `shots/qa1i-deck.mjs chromium`.
- **Expected:** FR-W28/W29 (`tasks/prd-names-pwa.md`): after one online visit the app works offline with the 3 background photos precached, so the deck keeps its photo and the background sheet shows its three photo thumbnails.
- **Actual:** The deck shows the plain canvas (the `<img>` for `/_next/image?url=/_next/static/media/names-fabric.….webp&w=1200&q=75` has `naturalWidth` 0), and the sheet's "מחברת", "בדים" and "חדר תינוק" thumbnails show broken-image icons. Cause: the worker precaches `/landing/names-*.webp`, but `ScreenBackground` and `BackgroundSheet` import the photos statically into `next/image`, so the page asks for `/_next/image?url=/_next/static/media/…&w=…` URLs that are only cached cache-first once fetched while the worker controls the page. After a second online visit only the exact deck width (w=1200) is cached; other widths and the thumbnails still miss. Simulator Safari (visited many times) showed the photo and the thumbnails offline, as expected for warm caches. Since #8 (the components were not touched in this run), so not a regression; it's a gap against FR-W28's "precache the 3 background photos" promise.
