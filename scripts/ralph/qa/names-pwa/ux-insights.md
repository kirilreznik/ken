# Names PWA: UX insights

Observations from QA that aren't bugs: friction, copy, ideas for later. Newest last.

| Cycle | Story | Screen | Insight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | US-QA1-A | sign-in/email (code step) | NP-UX-001: a long address wraps mid-address at a hyphen ("delivered+names-qa-" / "1@resend.dev") in "שלחנו קוד…". It is bdi-isolated so the order is right, but keeping the address on one line (or breaking only at "@") would read better. Screenshot shots/qa1-a-code-390.png. |
| 1 | US-QA1-B | intro, onboarding | NP-UX-002: at 390 × 844 both pages are ~70 px taller than the screen, so "התחלת בחירה" is cut off at the bottom until you scroll (board 1 crops it the same way). A sticky bottom button, or tighter spacing above the card art, would keep the main action in view. Screenshot shots/qa1-b-intro-top-390.png. |
| 1 | US-QA1-E | background sheet | NP-UX-003: once an own photo is saved, the sheet offers "החלפת התמונה" but no way to remove it from the device; the only route is picking another background (the Blob stays in IndexedDB) or signing out. A small "הסרת התמונה" link next to "החלפת התמונה" would let people take a private photo off a shared device. Screenshot shots/qa1-e-own-sheet-dark.png. |
| 1 | US-QA1-F | swipe (`/names`) | NP-UX-004: with only sheet filters on (letter, length, popularity, trend or origin), nothing on the swipe screen says a filter is active: "כל השמות" stays pressed and the "סינון" button has no dot or count. The only hint is the smaller "שם 1 מתוך n", or the empty-filter panel. A small dot on "סינון" (with "סינון, פעיל" as its label) would explain a short deck. Screenshot shots/qa1-f-empty-filter.png (letter ת + ארוך). |
| 1 | US-QA1-F | filters sheet | NP-UX-005: none of the 10 content names is rising (9 are יציב, עמית is בירידה), so "בעלייה" always leads to the empty-filter state. The data is right (it matches US-011 and the cards), but a chip that never shows anything can feel broken. This will matter less once more names are added. |
| 1 | US-QA1-G | add-name sheet | NP-UX-006: niqqud is stripped but the spelling is kept, so "נֹעַם" (defective spelling with holam) becomes a new custom name "נעם" instead of the data name נועם ("נוֹעַם" with the vav maps correctly). This is right by FR-W18's rules, but a short "האם התכוונת ל־נועם?" when the stripped name is one vav/yod away from a data name would stop a person from saving a copy of a name the deck already has, with no count. |
| 1 | US-QA1-H | join, invite | NP-UX-007: when the person who joins had their own solo names space with no votes, `names_join` removes that space, while the joined screen and the invite page say nothing about it. That's fine. But a person who joins by mistake has no way to leave the shared space from the UI (settings only has sign-out and account deletion). A "יציאה מהבחירה המשותפת" row in settings would make a join reversible. |
| 1 | US-QA1-I | duel result | NP-UX-008: after "עצירה" the toast "הדו־קרב נשמר. אפשר להמשיך מאותו מקום." stays up for 4 s and follows the person back into the duel. A quick resume to the result screen puts the toast over "חזרה לרשימה" (the toast is pointer-events-auto). Dismissing the toast when the duel reopens, or lifting it above the bottom actions, would keep the link usable. Screenshot shots/qa1-i-result.png. |
| 1 | US-QA1-J | privacy, terms | NP-UX-009: the version line reads "גרסה 2026-11-04-draft · עודכן ב־4 בנובמבר 2026". The Latin "draft" token in a Hebrew sentence repeats the banner above it and reads like an internal id. Showing only the date (or "טיוטה" in Hebrew) and keeping the raw version for `user_metadata` would read better. Screenshot shots/qa1-j-privacy-390.png. |
| 1 | US-QA1-L | every bottom sheet | NP-UX-010: each bottom sheet has two Tab stops both named "סגירה" (the drag handle and the X), back to back. Both work, but a keyboard or screen reader user meets the same control twice before the content. Taking the handle out of the Tab order (it stays a pointer target) and keeping the X would shorten every sheet's walk by one stop. |
